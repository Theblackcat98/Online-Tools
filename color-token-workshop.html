    Contrast audit

    Ratios are WCAG 2.1 contrast; Lc is the signed APCA (WCAG 3 draft) lightness-contrast score — positive for dark text on a light fill, negative for light text on a dark fill. Roughly, |Lc| 60+ suits body text, 45+ suits large text, headlines and UI components. A pair flagged APCA low passes WCAG but sits under the APCA level suggested for its use. Fixes target the WCAG AA threshold for each pair.
