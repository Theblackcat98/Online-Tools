What does and doesn't extrude: filled paths, rects, circles, and polygons become solid shapes (holes inside shapes are kept). Strokes have no area, so they're ignored — convert strokes to outlines first ("Path → Stroke to Path" in Inkscape). Text must be converted to paths too. Overlapping shapes are exported side-by-side; most slicers union them at slice time.
