PNG to SVG

Real vector tracing — not a PNG stuffed inside an SVG wrapper. Your image is quantized into color regions and each region is traced into editable <path> shapes. Best on logos, icons, and flat illustrations; photos trace, but you'll get thousands of paths and a big file.
